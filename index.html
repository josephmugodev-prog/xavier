About Our School

St. Xavier's High School is a private mixed-day school located opposite Mercy Mission Hospital, along Moi Road, Nakuru County, Kenya. The school provides an educational environment that encourages learners to grow academically, socially and spiritually.

Our Catholic Foundation

The school is owned and managed by the Catholic Diocese of Nakuru in collaboration with the Incarnate Word Sisters. Christian values guide the school's approach to education, discipline, respect and service.

Our Educational Mission

We believe that every learner has potential. Our educational approach seeks to develop intellectual ability, moral responsibility, confidence, creativity and a commitment to making a positive difference.

Academic Development

We encourage learners to explore their interests, develop their abilities and prepare for future opportunities through continuous learning.

Character Formation

Education includes more than classroom learning. We value kindness, honesty, discipline, responsibility, respect and compassion in everyday school life.

Community and Service

We recognise the importance of working together with families and the wider community to support learners and prepare them to serve society.

GETTING READY

Uniform & learner requirements.

Please find below the school uniform and personal requirements for learners. For clarification or further guidance, kindly contact the school administration.

Girls' uniform

  • 2 skirts
  • 2 trousers
  • 2 short-sleeved blouses/shirts
  • 2 ties
  • 1 long-sleeved sweater
  • 1 sleeveless sweater
  • 2 pairs of socks

Boys' uniform

  • 1 blazer
  • 1 tracksuit
  • 1 P.E. kit
  • T-shirt
  • Hymn book
  • Prayer book
Textbooks: Textbooks are available in the school library.

To be provided by learners

  • Black shoes for all students
  • White sports shoes
  • 8 ruled exercise books and 1 squared book (200 pages)
  • Biro pens and pencils
  • Music writing book (music students)
  • Scientific calculator and mathematical log table (STEM students)
  • Plate, cup and spoon
  • Revised Standard Bible for all
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Copy of KJSEA certificate
  • Padlock
Grooming: Girls should braid their hair and neatly tie it at the centre.
