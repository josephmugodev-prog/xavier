ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Education that shapes
the whole person.

Welcome to St. Xavier's High School, a learning community committed to education, personal growth and the development of responsible young people.

Our approach to education values academic development, spiritual growth, moral responsibility, discipline, respect and service to others.

We seek to provide an environment where learners can discover their abilities, build confidence and prepare for meaningful contributions to society.

Much in Little Our school motto

Knowledge

Encouraging curiosity, learning and the development of knowledge and practical skills.

Character

Promoting honesty, discipline, kindness, respect and responsibility.

Service

Preparing learners to participate positively in their communities and society.
